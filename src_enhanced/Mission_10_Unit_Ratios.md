## 📖 The Hunter's Knowledge (Brain Download)

### What is a Ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities:
```
Wins : Losses
Boys : Girls
Cost : Quantity
```

**Writing Ratios:**
- With a colon: 3:2 ("3 to 2")
- As a fraction: 3/2
- With words: "3 to 2"

### Unit Rates

A **unit rate** tells you the amount per ONE unit:
```
$75 for 15 hamburgers
Unit rate = $75 ÷ 15 = $5 per hamburger

120 miles in 2 hours
Unit rate = 120 ÷ 2 = 60 miles per hour
```

### Scaling Ratios

If the ratio is 3:2, you can scale it:
```
3:2 (original)
6:4 (× 2)
9:6 (× 3)
12:8 (× 4)

The relationship stays the same!
```

## 🗡️ The Quest (Activity)

### 📜 Verbatim Instructions:
Choose your favorite sport(s) or a variety to complete activities. Math can be found in many different sports. You may want to focus on one or more sports that your children are interested in and help them solve problems using that sport. Listed below are the areas that each grade should be working on and example problems for them to solve.

**Problem 1 - Basketball:**
A basketball team's season goes as follows:
1st game Loss; 2nd game Loss; 3rd game Win; 4th game Win; 5th game Win; 6th game Win; 7th game Win; 8th game Loss; 9th game Win; 10th game Loss; 11th game Loss; 12th game Win; 13th game Loss; 14th game Loss; 15th game Loss; 16th game Win.

What is the ratio of wins to losses?

**Problem 2 - Swimming:**
There are 2 swim teams at a swim meet. One team has 16 boys and 13 girls. The second team has 14 boys and 7 girls. What is the ratio of boys to girls at the swim meet?

**Problem 3 - Wrestling:**
A wrestler has a ratio of 3 wins for every 2 losses. If the wrestler has won 12 matches, how many matches has he lost? Then find out how many total matches he has wrestled.

**Problem 4 - Basketball Challenge:**
See how many baskets you can make in 10 attempts. What is your ratio of baskets made/attempts? How many baskets do you think you would make if you made 20 attempts? Try it. Did you get the same ratio?

### 💡 Hunter's Strategy Guide

**Problem 1: Basketball Season Analysis**
```
Count the games:
L L W W W W W L W L L W L L L W

Wins (W): 8
Losses (L): 8

Ratio of Wins to Losses = 8:8 = 1:1

The team won exactly half their games!
```

**Problem 2: Swim Meet**
```
Team 1: 16 boys + 13 girls
Team 2: 14 boys + 7 girls

Total Boys: 16 + 14 = 30
Total Girls: 13 + 7 = 20

Ratio of Boys to Girls = 30:20 = 3:2
```

**Problem 3: Wrestling Prediction**
```
Ratio: 3 wins for every 2 losses (3:2)

He won 12 matches.
12 ÷ 3 = 4 (the multiplier)

Losses: 2 × 4 = 8 matches lost

Total matches: 12 + 8 = 20 matches
```

**Problem 4: Your Challenge**
```
Shoot 10 baskets. Record:
Made: ___
Missed: ___

Ratio: Made : Attempts = ___ : 10

Predict for 20 attempts: ___

Try 20 more! Was your prediction right?
```
