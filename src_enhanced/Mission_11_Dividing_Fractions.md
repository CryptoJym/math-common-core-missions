## 📖 The Hunter's Knowledge (Brain Download)

### The Secret Technique: Keep, Change, Flip!

To divide fractions, follow these three steps:
```
2/3 ÷ 1/2 = ?

Step 1: KEEP the first fraction    → 2/3
Step 2: CHANGE ÷ to ×              → ×
Step 3: FLIP the second fraction   → 2/1

2/3 × 2/1 = 4/3 = 1⅓
```

### Why Does This Work?

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal:
```
Dividing by 1/2 means: "How many halves fit in here?"

In 2/3 of a pizza, there is 1⅓ halves.
```

### Visual Understanding

```
Original pizza: 2/3 remaining
┌───┬───┬───┐
│ ▓ │ ▓ │   │
└───┴───┴───┘
  2/3 shaded

Question: How many 1/2 portions fit in 2/3?

Answer: 1 full half + 1/3 of a half = 1⅓
```

Ask the students if they had ⅔ of a pizza and they divide the ⅔ by ½ how many pieces would they have? Give other similar problems such as ¾ ÷ ¼ ; ⅖ ÷ ½

**Family Pizza Night Challenge:**
Your family got 4 kinds of pizza for dinner last night. 1/3 of the pineapple, 1/4 of the pepperoni and 1/2 of the cheese is left. You want to split each kind in 1/2 with your brother. How much pizza will you get?

---

### 💡 Hunter's Strategy Guide

**Problem 1: ⅔ ÷ ½**
```
Keep:   2/3
Change: ÷ → ×
Flip:   1/2 → 2/1

2/3 × 2/1 = 4/3 = 1⅓

Answer: You would have 1⅓ pieces
```

**Problem 2: ¾ ÷ ¼**
```
Keep:   3/4
Change: ÷ → ×
Flip:   1/4 → 4/1

3/4 × 4/1 = 12/4 = 3

Answer: 3 pieces (because 3 quarters contain 3 quarter-portions)
```

**Problem 3: ⅖ ÷ ½**
```
Keep:   2/5
Change: ÷ → ×
Flip:   1/2 → 2/1

2/5 × 2/1 = 4/5

Answer: 4/5 of a piece
```

**Family Pizza Challenge:**
```
Leftover pizza portions, split with brother:

Pineapple: 1/3 ÷ 2 = 1/3 × 1/2 = 1/6
Pepperoni: 1/4 ÷ 2 = 1/4 × 1/2 = 1/8
Cheese:    1/2 ÷ 2 = 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/4

YOU get: 1/6 pineapple + 1/8 pepperoni + 1/4 cheese
```
