## 📖 The Hunter's Knowledge (Brain Download)

### The Vocabulary of Expressions

**TERM** - Parts of an expression separated by + or - signs:
```
In 5x + 3y - 2:
• 5x is a term
• 3y is a term
• 2 is a term
There are 3 terms total.
```

**COEFFICIENT** - The number multiplied by a variable:
```
In 5x:
• 5 is the coefficient
• x is the variable

In 3y + 7:
• 3 is the coefficient of y
• 7 is a constant (no variable attached)
```

**FACTOR** - Numbers you multiply together:
```
In 6 × 4 = 24:
• 6 is a factor
• 4 is a factor
• 24 is the product
```

### Operation Results

| Operation | Answer Name |
|-----------|-------------|
| Addition | **SUM** |
| Subtraction | **DIFFERENCE** |
| Multiplication | **PRODUCT** |
| Division | **QUOTIENT** |

**Examples:**
```
8 + 5 = 13     → 13 is the SUM
10 - 3 = 7    → 7 is the DIFFERENCE
4 × 6 = 24    → 24 is the PRODUCT
72 ÷ 9 = 8    → 8 is the QUOTIENT
```

## 🗡️ The Quest (Activity)

### 📜 Verbatim Instructions:
Today we are going to make a pretend store. Have them make a store with their favorite toys. The different age groups will need to have appropriate prices for their age group. Set up the store in advance.

Go to your store and figure out the following:
- The sum (the answer when you add them) if you buy two of the objects.
- The difference (the answer when you subtract) between how much money you have and how much you will need if you buy those two items.
- The product (the answer when you multiply) if you buy multiples of one item.
- Find the quotient (the answer when you divide) to figure out how much each item cost if you spent 72 cents and you bought nine of them?
- Write an equation to represent what you did that includes coefficients, factors and terms.

### 💡 Hunter's Strategy Guide

**Phase 1: Set Up Your Store**
```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════╗
║        🏪 THE TOY DUNGEON 🏪           ║
╠═══════════════════════════════════════╣
║  🧸 Teddy Bear ............. $5.00    ║
║  🚗 Toy Car ................ $3.00    ║
║  🎮 Action Figure .......... $7.00    ║
║  📚 Book ................... $4.00    ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════╝
```

**Phase 2: Find the SUM**
```
Buy 2 items: Teddy Bear + Toy Car
$5.00 + $3.00 = $8.00

The SUM is $8.00
```

**Phase 3: Find the DIFFERENCE**
```
You have $10.00
You need $8.00 (from above)

$10.00 - $8.00 = $2.00

The DIFFERENCE is $2.00
```

**Phase 4: Find the PRODUCT**
```
Buy 5 Toy Cars at $3.00 each

5 × $3.00 = $15.00

The PRODUCT is $15.00
```

**Phase 5: Find the QUOTIENT**
```
You spent 72 cents on 9 items
How much did each cost?

72 ÷ 9 = 8 cents each

The QUOTIENT is 8
```

**Phase 6: Write Your Equation**
```
Example: 5c + t = total

Where:
• 5 is the coefficient
• c is the variable (car price)
• t is a term (teddy bear price)
• "5c" and "t" are both terms
```
